Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a DJ cost?

Our DJ services start at $800 for 6 hours. Pricing varies based on event type, duration, and additional services like photography or uplighting.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes! We offer flexible payment plans. A 50% deposit secures your date, with the balance due one week before your event.

What areas do you service?

We serve the entire DMV area within a 70-mile radius of Woodbridge, VA, including Washington DC, Northern Virginia, and Maryland.

Can you customize packages?

Absolutely! Every event is unique. We'll work with you to create a custom package that fits your needs and budget.

What if I need to reschedule?

We understand plans change. We offer flexible rescheduling options up to 30 days before your event.

What is included in the package duration?

All packages include 6 hours of service. Anything over 6 hours is an extra $200 per hour.
